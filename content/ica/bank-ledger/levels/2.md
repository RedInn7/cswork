Rank the accounts by how much money left them.

| Method | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `top_outgoing(timestamp, n)` | up to `n` strings `"<account_id>(<total>)"`, e.g. `["ben(300)", "cy(250)"]` |

- An account's outgoing total is the sum of its successful withdrawals and the transfers it sent. Deposits and money received do not count, nor does any call that returned `None`.
- Every existing account is ranked, including those whose total is 0.
- Larger totals first; equal totals by account id in ascending string order.
- With fewer than `n` accounts, return them all.

Public tests: `tests/test_level2.py`. The hidden tests of level 1 run again with this level's.
