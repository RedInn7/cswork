Accounts can be merged, and their past balances looked up.

| Method | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `merge(timestamp, keep_id, absorb_id)` | `True` after merging `absorb_id` into `keep_id`; `False` if either account is missing or both ids are the same |
| `balance_at(timestamp, account_id, time_at)` | the account's balance at time `time_at` (`time_at ≤ timestamp`), or `None` |

**Merging**

- `keep_id`'s balance and outgoing total grow by `absorb_id`'s.
- `absorb_id` stops existing: calls naming it return `None` or `False`, it is no longer ranked, and the id can never be opened again.
- Scheduled transfers that have not run and involve `absorb_id` now involve `keep_id` (cancel them with `keep_id`). One that has not run between the two accounts is cancelled.

**Past balances**

- The balance at time T is the balance after everything that happened at times ≤ T. A scheduled transfer happens at its due time, although it runs at the start of a later call.
- Before the account was opened: `None`.
- `keep_id` before the merge time: its own balance then. From the merge time on: including what it absorbed.
- `absorb_id` before the merge time: **its own balance then**, as if it had never been merged. From the merge time on: `None`.
