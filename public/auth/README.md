# Google sign-in icon

`google-g.png` is the unmodified Google G asset from Google's official sign-in
branding guidelines, downloaded 2026-09-12. Google retains its trademark rights.

- Guidelines: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
- Asset: https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png

Keep its original colors and 200:204 aspect ratio. It is served locally, displayed
on white, and accompanied by the visible “使用 Google 登录” button label.
